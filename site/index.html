Why “Wherefore”

wherefore adverb

for what reason; why.

Juliet wasn’t asking where Romeo was. She was asking why he had to be who he was.

In a Wherefore repository every file has to answer her: generated from a source, written by a person for a stated reason, or captured and stamped with what it was made from. A file that cannot answer stops the change.

Case study · Chaotic Pendulums

A hardware company, compiled from one spec file.

Chaotic Pendulums is a small hardware company our founder started to test this idea on something with more surface area than software: a desk-sized double pendulum with milled parts, a bronze blade, bearings and a clamp. Wherefore was built alongside it, one rule at a time. If one source can hold machined parts, physics, suppliers and a storefront, it can hold your app, website and docs. One spec file holds the object. Everything a shop, a customer or a printer receives is built from it, and checked on every push.

See the live site

What it compiles

  • Part drawings a machine shop quotes and inspects against, each with its cut file and its solid model
  • Print-ready 3D files for a prototype, laid out plate by plate
  • A bill of materials, and a quote bundle a shop’s online tool takes in one drag
  • The engineering hand-over, frozen at every release so a shop can always find the pack it quoted
  • The brand: the mark, the hero and the renders, drawn from the object’s own dimensions
  • The website, and the 3D pendulum that swings on it

What holds it

  • Load simulations, worked from the real parts’ masses, prove every mount setup holds the pendulum at a torque a hand can give.
  • Worst-case tolerance stacks prove every screw clamps and never bottoms, at every extreme of every band.
  • A sweep proves the blade turns a full circle and touches nothing.
  • The swing on the website is integrated from the physics of the object as it is today, and a check holds every mass and length to the parts.
  • Move any dimension, and a probe requires every file a supplier receives to move with it, or the change is refused.
  • 49 checks, each with a proof that plants the fault it exists to catch
  • 9 parts, each drawn, cut and modelled from the spec
  • 5 print plates for a prototype anyone holding the pack can make

Counted at V83 of the pack, on 29 October 2026.
